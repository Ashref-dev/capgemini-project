# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IntelliConnect serves Capgemini Tunisia employees and external partners. Employees use it during operational work to manage partners, contacts, offers, documents, events, HR workflows, analytics, reports, and AI-assisted partner intelligence. Partners use it to access their own portal, offers, documents, notifications, statistics, recruitments, and supplier projects.

Users are usually trying to complete work quickly, compare information, take action, and trust that the system reflects real data. The interface should reduce cognitive load and make the next action obvious.

## Product Purpose

IntelliConnect centralizes the partnership lifecycle for Capgemini Tunisia, from public partnership requests through employee review, partner management, business intelligence, and AI-supported decision-making.

Success means the product is usable, stable, fast, and clear enough to support a final internship project demonstration while also feeling credible as an internal Capgemini tool.

## Brand Personality

Clean, precise, and professional.

The product should feel calm, organized, minimal, and functional. It should communicate enterprise trust without becoming cold or generic. Micro-interactions should make state changes feel responsive, not decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid inconsistent hardcoded color usage, generic AI-generated SaaS visuals, noisy glassmorphism, oversized decorative hero sections on task surfaces, nested cards, excessive shadows, blue-purple gradients, and marketing-page composition inside authenticated product workflows.

Avoid layouts where the user must scroll past decorative content before reaching the task. Avoid form pages with unnecessary footers, large empty headers, or narrow cards that waste desktop space.

## Design Principles

1. Task first: every screen should make the user’s current job faster and clearer.
2. Token discipline: colors, spacing, focus states, and surfaces come from the shared design system.
3. Quiet confidence: use restraint, hierarchy, and readable density instead of decoration.
4. State completeness: loading, error, empty, success, disabled, hover, focus, and validation states are part of the design.
5. French UX, English code: all user-facing copy is French; code and documentation remain English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility for all interactive flows. Support light and dark mode equally. Respect reduced motion preferences by keeping animation subtle, short, and state-driven. Do not rely on color alone for status or validation feedback.
