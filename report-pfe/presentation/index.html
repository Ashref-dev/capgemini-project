Context05 / 40

Partnership work was spread across disconnected surfaces.

The difficulty was not the absence of data. It was the cost of connecting partner records, documents, activities, projects, and decisions at the moment they were needed.

01

Scattered information

Partner profiles, contacts, offers, documents, and project evidence lived in separate operational paths.

02

Manual follow-up

Requests, commitments, meetings, and lifecycle changes required repeated human reconciliation.

03

Weak decision traceability

Scores, recommendations, and reports needed a visible evidence base instead of unsupported summaries.

Research questionHow can a governed platform centralise partnership operations while making AI-assisted analysis verifiable?
Context06 / 40

Build a partnership platform that keeps business data, workflow, and analysis connected.

  1. 01
    Centralise

    Partners, projects, documents, offers, events, and requests share one domain model.

  2. 02
    Structure

  3. 03
    Assist

Analysis14 / 40

A partnership becomes traceable through explicit states.

The target process connects public intake, employee qualification, an accountable decision, activation, and operational collaboration.

01Request

Public visitor submits a structured partnership request.

02Qualify

Employee validates completeness, fit, and ownership.

03Decide

Accept, reject, or open negotiation with a visible reason.

04Activate

Create the managed relationship, category, status, and scope.

05Operate

Track offers, events, documents, projects, and decisions.

The system preserves the transition history instead of treating the current status as the whole story.
